Tree removal FAQs

Clear answers before removal.

How do I know whether Cardinal can remove my tree?

Send us the address, photos if available, and a description of the tree. We can quickly determine whether it fits our scope or requires a specialized tree-removal contractor.

Do you remove the debris?

Debris removal and cleanup can be included in the agreed estimate so you know what to expect before work starts.

Can you improve the area after a tree is removed?

Yes. Because Cardinal also provides landscaping, we can discuss cleanup, bed renewal, planting or other improvements after suitable removals.
