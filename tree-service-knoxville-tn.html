When to call

Signs your trees or shrubs need attention.

Most Knoxville homeowners wait longer than they should. These are the signs it's time to schedule tree service:

  • Branches hanging over the roof, gutters, or a fence line
  • Trees or shrubs blocking light to windows or the front of the home
  • Overgrown hedges that have lost their shape
  • Dead or crossing branches that create debris after every storm
  • Ornamental trees that haven't been pruned in two or more years
  • Shrubs that have grown into walkways or driveways

If you're unsure whether a job is within our scope, call or send photos. We'll tell you directly — and if a project requires a licensed arborist or specialized equipment, we'll say so rather than take on work we shouldn't.

Tree service FAQs

Common questions before we get started.

Do you provide free tree service estimates in Knoxville?

Yes. Contact Cardinal with your address and a description of the work — or send photos to help us understand the scope. We'll confirm availability and provide a clear estimate before any work begins.

What's the difference between trimming and pruning?

Trimming is mainly about appearance — cutting back branches that have grown out of shape or too far. Pruning focuses on the health of the tree: removing dead, diseased, or crossing branches so the tree grows better. Cardinal does both, and we'll tell you which your trees need when we take a look.

Do you haul away the debris after trimming?

Yes. Branch and debris cleanup is included in the agreed project scope. We don't leave brush piles for you to deal with.

Can you handle large or hazardous tree removals?

Some large, dead, storm-damaged, or high-risk trees require specialized rigging equipment or a licensed arborist. We'll tell you directly when a project exceeds our scope rather than take it on and create a safety issue. If you need a specialist, we'll say so upfront.

How often should trees be trimmed in East Tennessee?

Most ornamental and shade trees benefit from trimming every 2–3 years. Fast-growing species or trees near structures may need attention more often. During your estimate, we can give you a straightforward recommendation for what your specific trees need.

Do you work on shrubs and hedges as well as trees?

Yes. Shrub shaping, hedge trimming, and overgrown foundation plantings are a regular part of our work. If your beds or hedges have gotten out of hand, that's something we can address alongside any tree work.

What areas around Knoxville do you serve?

We serve Knoxville and surrounding communities throughout East Tennessee, including Farragut, West Knoxville, Bearden, Oak Ridge, Maryville, Powell, and Halls. Call us with your address and we'll quickly confirm availability.
